Quick Start Guide

Step 1 of 4: Hybrid Calculation Format

Hybrid Multi-Topic Problems

To mirror real test conditions, questions are never isolated. They combine 2 to 3 topics at once (e.g. Factorials with Percentages, Powers with Multiplication Tables).

Example: (4! × 3) + (37.5% of 240) = 162
Scoring & Dynamic Ideal Time

Each sprint rewards +3 for correct answers and penalizes -1 for incorrect attempts.

Every question features an objective Ideal Time benchmark (typically 5s–11s) dynamically evaluated from the exact mental operations required.

Pacing Controls & Shortcuts

• TITA Mode: Type your answer and press Enter (optimized for mobile numeric keypad).
• MCQ Mode: Click or press hotkeys A, B, C, D.
• Pause: Hit Space or click the Pause button anytime.
