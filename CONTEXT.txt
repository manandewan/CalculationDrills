1. PROJECT PURPOSE & CORE PHILOSOPHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- "Knowing concepts is great, but everything in competitive exams (CAT, XAT, DILR, GMAT) 
  relies on your raw speed and ability to arrive at the final numerical answer."
- 100% PURE mental calculation drills. Zero word problem baggage, zero theoretical geometry/QA fluff.
- High-yield, exam-realistic numbers: Prioritize numbers and fractions that actually appear 
  in 90% of real CAT/DILR questions rather than obscure edge cases.
- Immediate, high-contrast feedback loop: Real exam scoring (+3 for correct, -1 for wrong, 0 for skip).

3. USER PREFERENCES & STRICT DESIGN RULES (WHAT WE LIKE & DISLIKE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[LIKED & MANDATED]:
✓ Real CAT High-Yield Clustering:
  - 90% of exam fractions cluster near 50%: 5/12 (41.67%), 7/12 (58.33%), 7/15 (46.67%), 8/15 (53.33%), 3/8, 5/8, 7/8, 2/7, 4/7, 5/7.
  - Difficulty Scaling: 90% Tier-1 on Easy, 80% on Normal, 55% on Hard (with 45% extended).
✓ Mental Division by Primes:
  - Heavily weighted towards primes under 10 (3 and 7 at 70%), with remaining 30% for 11, 13, 17, 19.
✓ Multi-Term Long Addition & Subtraction:
  - 4 to 5 term mixed arithmetic with 2-digit and 3-digit speed numbers (e.g. 84 - 37 + 65 + 49 - 28, 380 + 265 - 190 + 85).
✓ Dynamic Pacing Targets (Ideal Time):
  - Every problem computes realistic target seconds based on operator complexity (e.g. 2.0s for base table, 5.5s for hybrid fraction + product).
✓ Clean Untimed Mistake Practice:
  - Reattempting mistakes must be 100% untimed. The "Time Remaining" timer HUD is completely hidden, and reattempts NEVER pollute history records.
✓ Ergonomic Keyboard Shortcuts:
  - Enter to submit TITA, A/B/C/D or 1/2/3/4 for MCQ, H for Hint, Spacebar for Pause, Escape to close modals.

[DISLIKED & FORBIDDEN]:
✗ NO obscure, non-tested edge cases (e.g. multi-numerators of high primes like 7/19, 8/17, 11/13 are banned).
✗ NO trivial questions like 50% of 80 or 10% of 60 in speed drills.
✗ NO hints on basic multiplication tables (19 × 7 needs zero hints; hints reserved for identities/powers/fractions).
✗ NO over-revealing hints on factorials (show base definition like "Use 7! = 5040", never pre-calculate the quotient).
✗ NO including paused time in question duration or Longest Solve (Time Sinks).
✗ NO saving 0-question aborted sprints to History.

4. DUAL-LAYER ANTI-REPETITION & PERMUTATION ENGINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Level 1 (Exact Signature Buffer - 35 items):
   Prevents identical questions from repeating within any 35-question sprint window.
2. Level 2 (Family-Level Base Buffer - 10 items):
   Tracks the underlying base element (e.g. `sq_19`, `cb_14`, `frac_5/12`, `tbl_17`, `divprime_7`).
   Even if the additive offset changes, the same root number will not appear twice within 10 questions.
3. Algebraic Permutation Diversity:
   - Alternates squaring templates: (X² + offset), (X² - offset), and (offset - X²).
   - Fraction multiples rotate across 15 distinct scaling factors (3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 12, 14, 15, 16, 18, 20, 24, 25).
   - Division & Products use varied midpoints (25, 30, 35, 40, 50, 60) and twin product spreads.

5. TOPIC BREAKDOWN & GENERATOR POOLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Powers:
   - Squares (14² to 32²), Cubes (7³ to 16³), Base-2 (2¹–2¹²), Base-3 (3¹–3⁷), Base-4 (4¹–4⁶), Base-5 (5¹–5⁶), Base-6 (6¹–6⁵), Base-7 (7¹–7⁴).
2. Fractions & Percentages:
   - High-yield denominators (3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12, 14, 15, 16, 18, 20) with fraction-of-integer and percentage-reversals.
3. Factorials:
   - High-yield ratios (e.g., 8!/6!, 7!/5!) and factorial arithmetic offsets.
4. Tables & Products:
   - CAT tables (17, 19, 23, 24, 26, 29), Twin Products (a-b)(a+b), and Prime Divisions (3, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19).
5. Addition & Subtraction:
   - 4-5 term mixed (+ and -), 3-digit speed arithmetic, and DILR column totals.
6. Roots & Decimals:
   - Square roots (√2, √3, √5, √6, √7, √8, √10), Cube roots (∛2, ∛3), and CAT Decimal Multipliers (0.375, 0.625, 0.875, 1.25, 1.333, 1.667).
7. Hybrid Combinations:
   - High-yield multi-step problems blending fractions, powers, and tables.
