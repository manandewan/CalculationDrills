1. PROJECT PURPOSE & CORE PHILOSOPHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- "Knowing concepts is great, but everything in competitive exams (CAT, XAT, DILR, GMAT) 
  relies on your raw speed and ability to arrive at the final numerical answer."
- 100% PURE mental calculation drills. Zero word problem baggage, zero theoretical geometry/QA fluff.
- High-yield, exam-realistic numbers: Prioritize numbers and fractions that actually appear 
  in 90% of real CAT/DILR questions rather than obscure edge cases.
- Immediate, high-contrast feedback loop: Real exam scoring (+3 for correct, -1 for wrong, 0 for skip).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CORE QUIZZING & QUESTION GENERATION ENGINE (WHAT WAS INTENDED & DESIGNED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. "Anchors of the Sprint" Protocol:
   - At the beginning of each sprint (`initSessionAnchors()`), the app silently selects 3 target concept anchors:
     1. One High-Yield Square (e.g. 19², 23², 28², 29²).
     2. One High-Yield Multiplication Table (e.g. 17, 19, 23, 26, 28, 29).
     3. One High-Yield Fraction-to-Percentage Anchor (e.g. 5/12, 7/12, 7/15, 8/15, 3/8, 5/8, 7/8, 2/7, 5/7).
   - These 3 anchors are woven into diverse arithmetic operations repeatedly throughout the sprint 
     (e.g., as sq² + offset, sq² - offset, offset - sq², (sq² × mult) + offset, (f.p of base) + table, 
     dividend ÷ table + offset) so you are forced to retrieve them from memory 3 to 4 times per sprint.

B. Dual-Layer Anti-Repetition Cache:
   - Level 1: 35-question signature memory buffer prevents exact duplicate questions from recurring in a sprint.
   - Level 2: 6-family buffer prevents back-to-back testing of the exact same number while allowing the 
     sprint anchors to rotate smoothly every 5–7 questions across varied operations.

C. High-Yield Real Exam Clustering:
   - 90% of exam fractions cluster near 50%: 5/12 (41.67%), 7/12 (58.33%), 7/15 (46.67%), 8/15 (53.33%), 
     3/8 (37.5%), 5/8 (62.5%), 7/8 (87.5%), 2/7 (28.57%), 4/7 (57.14%), 5/7 (71.42%), 5/6 (83.33%), 
     4/9 (44.44%), 7/9 (77.77%), 3/11 (27.27%), 7/11 (63.63%).
   - Weighted distribution by difficulty: 90% Tier-1 on Easy, 80% on Normal, 55% on Hard (45% extended).
   - Multiplication by 18/5 with multiples of 5 and 5/18 with multiples of 18 (km/h ↔ m/s factor drills).

D. Mental Division by Primes:
   - Heavily weighted towards primes under 10 (3 and 7 at 70%), with remaining 30% for 11, 13, 17, 19.

E. Long Multi-Term Addition & Subtraction:
   - 4 to 5 term mixed arithmetic with 2-digit and 3-digit speed numbers (e.g. 84 - 37 + 65 + 49 - 28, 380 + 265 - 190 + 85).
   - 4 to 6 term DILR cumulative column totals.

F. Multi-Topic Hybrids (Never in Isolation):
   - Hybrids across 2–3 topics (Powers + Tables, Fractions + Tables, Factorials + Fractions) with addition/subtraction.

G. Dynamic Ideal Time:
   - Unique target seconds evaluated dynamically per question based on operator complexity.

H. Hints & Solutions:
   - Hints only for algebraic identities/powers/roots/factorials/fractions. NEVER for basic tables (19 × 7).
   - Factorial hints only state the definition (e.g., "Use 7! = 5040"), never pre-calculating the quotient.
   - Every question displays full step-by-step solution shortcut on results review.

3. USER PREFERENCES & STRICT UX RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[MANDATED]:
✓ Untimed Reattempts: Mistake practice is 100% untimed, hides countdown HUD, and never saves to History.

✓ Clean History: 0-question aborted sprints are discarded from localStorage.

✗ NO trivial questions (50% of 80 or 10% of 60).
